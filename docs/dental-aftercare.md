# Dental Cleaning & Extraction After-Care Instructions

## First 24 Hours

- Expect grogginess and mild wobbliness from anesthesia.
- Keep your pet calm and in a quiet space.
- Offer water once fully awake and alert.

## Eating & Diet

- Feed **soft food** for the vet-directed period (commonly **7–10 days** after extractions).
- Soften dry kibble with warm water if needed.
- **Avoid** hard treats, chews, bones, and toys while the mouth heals.
- Appetite may be reduced the first day — this is normal.

## Mouth Care

- Do **not** brush teeth or touch the extraction sites until the vet approves.
- A small amount of blood-tinged saliva on the first day can be normal.
- Bad breath, mild drooling, or pawing at the mouth may occur briefly.

**Call the vet if you see:** ongoing bleeding, swelling of the face or gums, refusal to eat beyond 48 hours, foul discharge, or signs of pain.

## Medication

- Give antibiotics and pain medication exactly as prescribed.
- **Never** give human pain relievers — many are toxic to pets.

## Activity

- Keep activity calm for the first 24 hours.
- Resume normal routine once your pet is alert and eating comfortably.
