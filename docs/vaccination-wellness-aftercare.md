# Vaccination & Wellness Visit After-Care Instructions

## Normal Reactions (usually within about 24 hours)

Mild reactions are common, start within hours, and usually pass on their own within about a day:

- Soreness, redness, or mild swelling at the injection site
- Mild tiredness or reduced activity
- Slightly reduced appetite
- Low-grade fever
- After a vaccine given in the nose: sneezing, a runny nose, or a mild cough for a few days

Give your pet a calm, quiet place to rest and keep other pets from bothering them.

**Call the vet if** mild reactions last longer than a day or two, or your pet seems very uncomfortable.

## Lump at the Injection Site

- A small, firm lump under the skin is common and should shrink and go away within a couple of weeks.
- **Call the vet if the lump** lasts longer than 3 weeks, keeps growing, or becomes painful.

## Food, Water & Activity

- Keep fresh water available.
- Offer normal meals; a smaller appetite for a day is okay.
- Keep activity light for the first 24 hours, then resume the normal routine.

## Call the Vet Right Away If You See

These can signal an allergic reaction. They can start within minutes to hours of a vaccine and need **emergency** care:

- Swelling of the face, muzzle, eyes, or neck
- Hives (small, raised, itchy red bumps)
- Persistent vomiting or diarrhea
- Persistent, severe coughing
- Difficulty breathing
- Weakness, fainting, or collapse

Before future vaccines, tell your vet about any reaction, even a mild one.
