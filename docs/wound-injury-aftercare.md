# Wound & Injury After-Care Instructions

## Wound Care Basics

- Check the wound **twice daily** for changes.
- Keep the area **clean and dry**.
- Prevent licking, biting, and scratching — use an e-collar ("cone") or recovery suit.
- Only clean or apply products the vet has approved.

**Normal healing:** mild redness, slight swelling, and a scab forming.

## Bandage Care (if applied)

- Keep the bandage **clean and dry** — cover it during walks or outdoors.
- Do not let your pet chew or pull at it.
- Watch for a bandage that slips, tightens, or gets wet.

**Call the vet if the bandage:** smells bad, is soaked through, causes swelling of toes/limb, or the pet seems in pain.

## Activity

- Restrict running, jumping, and rough play until healed.
- Leash walks for bathroom breaks only for dogs; confine cats and small pets indoors.
- No baths or swimming until the vet approves.

## Medication

- Give antibiotics and pain medication exactly as prescribed.
- **Never** give human pain relievers — many are toxic to pets.

## Call the Vet If You See

- Fresh bleeding or discharge/pus
- Increasing swelling, redness, or heat
- Foul odor from the wound
- The wound opening or sutures coming loose
- Fever, lethargy, or refusal to eat
- Signs of pain (whining, hiding, guarding the area)
