# Spay / Neuter After-Care Instructions

## First 24 Hours

- Keep your pet **warm, quiet, and confined** to a small space.
- Grogginess and wobbliness from anesthesia are normal.
- Offer a small amount of water, then a light meal (about half the usual portion).
- Supervise around stairs, furniture, other pets, and children.

## Activity Restrictions (usually 10–14 days)

- No running, jumping, or rough play.
- Dogs: short leash walks for bathroom breaks only.
- Cats & small pets: keep confined indoors; avoid high perches and climbing.
- No baths or swimming until the incision is fully healed.

## Incision Care

- Check the incision **twice a day**.
- Prevent licking or chewing — keep the e-collar ("cone") or recovery suit on at all times.
- Keep the area clean and dry; do not apply any ointment unless prescribed.

**Normal:** slight redness, minor swelling, small bruising.

**Call the vet if you see:** bleeding, discharge, pus, a foul smell, the incision opening, or missing sutures.

## Pain & Medication

- Give all medication exactly as prescribed.
- **Never** give human pain relievers (ibuprofen, acetaminophen, aspirin) — they can be toxic to pets.

## Call the Vet Immediately If

- No urination for more than 24 hours
- Repeated vomiting or diarrhea
- Not eating for more than 48 hours
- Pale gums, labored breathing, or collapse
- Severe lethargy or clear signs of pain
